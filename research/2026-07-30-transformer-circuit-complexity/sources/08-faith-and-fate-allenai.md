Content: Large-scale Transformers like ChatGPT and GPT-4 fail at surprisingly trivial problems despite being hailed as "sparks of AGI". Humans can solve 3-digit by 3-digit multiplication after learning basic rules; GPT-4 only achieves 59% accuracy. The paper argues transformers solve compositional tasks by reducing multi-step reasoning into linearized subgraph matching, without necessarily developing systematic problem-solving skills, and accuracy rapidly decays with problem complexity/depth.

Key takeaway: empirical validation that failure on multi-digit multiplication/composition tasks correlates with increasing serial depth — matches the circuit complexity prediction.
