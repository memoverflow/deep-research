Abstract: Characterizing neural networks in terms of better-understood formal systems has yielded insights. For transformers, this remains an active research area. This work identifies a variant of first-order logic with counting quantifiers (FOC[+;MOD]) that is simultaneously an upper bound for fixed-precision transformer encoders and a lower bound for transformer encoders — a tighter characterization than Merrill & Sabharwal's uniform TC0 upper bound. Shows FOC[+;MOD] is strictly less expressive than uniform TC0.

Key takeaway: refines the TC0 upper bound to a tighter logic-based characterization.
