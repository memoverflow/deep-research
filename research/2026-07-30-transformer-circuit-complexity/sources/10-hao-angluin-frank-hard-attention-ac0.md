Referenced via secondary sources: proves hard-attention transformers can be simulated by constant-depth AND/OR circuits, placing them in AC0 — a proper subset of TC0. This is the paper that motivated the "saturated attention" generalization (Merrill, Sabharwal & Smith 2022) which showed saturated attention escapes AC0 but stays within TC0.

Key takeaway: hard attention ⊆ AC0 (weaker than TC0); establishes the AC0/TC0 distinction central to the article's narrative.
