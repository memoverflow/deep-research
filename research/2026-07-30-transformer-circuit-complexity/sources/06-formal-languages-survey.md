Abstract: As transformers have gained prominence in NLP, researchers have investigated theoretically what problems they can and cannot solve, by treating problems as formal languages. This is a comprehensive survey documenting diverse assumptions underlying different results and providing a unified framework for harmonizing seemingly contradictory findings. Covers AC0, TC0, ACC0 circuit classes and how various attention regimes (hard, soft, saturated) map to them.

Key takeaway: unifying reference survey (TACL) for the whole circuit-complexity-of-transformers literature.
