## Key Content
Formalizes and generalizes Feldman's long-tail argument beyond classification: constructs natural prediction problems where *every* sufficiently accurate learning algorithm must encode, in the trained model, essentially all information about a large subset of its training examples — even information seemingly irrelevant to the prediction task (e.g. incidental PII co-occurring in training text). Provides the first rigorous information-theoretic (mutual-information-based) formalization of memorization later adapted by Morris et al. (2025) — the Meta paper explicitly credits this statistical/mutual-information definition of memorization as its starting point, before extending it to a per-instance Kolmogorov-complexity based measure suitable for single trained models.
